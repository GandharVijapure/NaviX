About NaviX

NaviX is built for large public gatherings where thousands of people converge and mobile networks struggle to keep up. Volunteers carrying ESP32 + LoRa + GPS devices form a mesh network that keeps emergency coordination alive even when cellular coverage collapses. This demo simulates that hardware layer end-to-end so the full workflow — SOS to resolution — can be shown without physical devices.
